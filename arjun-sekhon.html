About Arjun

Arjun graduated with a Juris Doctor degree from the University of Windsor in 2021, and was called to the Bar of Ontario in 2022. He has an undergraduate degree in Justice Studies from the University of Guelph, alongside a diploma in Community & Justice Involvement. While obtaining his law degree, Arjun held a Research Assistant position for Sports Law, acted as the Vice President of the Mining Law Society, and received the Dorothy Waddicor Memorial Award in Alternative Dispute Resolution.

Arjun advocates for clients in the areas of Corporate, Commercial, Criminal, Family, and Immigration law, including applications for Judicial Review. His particular interest in Criminal law led to his membership with The Criminal Justice Honour Society (Alpha Phi Sigma), and involvement with Community Legal Aid in Windsor, Ontario, where he argued cases before the Ontario Court of Justice and the Ontario Superior Court of Justice. Prior to forming Lakeshore Law, Arjun had been working on everything from setting up and running contract processes to acting as counsel for mergers, acquisitions, and divestitures in Toronto, Ontario.

Arjun leverages his deep hybrid experiences to provide timely and practical advice to clients.

Community Involvement

Outside the office, Arjun enjoys competing in duathlon events, spending time with his family, and volunteering with organizations such as the Ability Online Support Network. He is an active Board Member with Community Living, providing consultation for city planning, governance matters, and accessibility.
